## 2. Event positioning

### The annual international retreat

The Global Opportunity AI Summit is built for people who want more than information. It brings the AI Academy Bangladesh and AI Academy Australia communities together with founders, consultants, AI practitioners, and business leaders.

The programme is designed to create high-energy, trusted conversations that continue beyond the event itself.

### Programme pillars

| Pillar | What participants can expect |
| --- | --- |
| Learn | Business-ready AI, brand building, automation, and practical AI SaaS applications. |
| Source | AI SaaS, digital products, and services to use, promote, distribute, or apply in eCommerce. |
| Connect | Global experts, consultants, leaders, and potential future business partners. |
| Explore | Malaysia's business ecosystem, Genting Highlands, and Kuala Lumpur. |
